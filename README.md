## Project Overview
The **Digital Literacy Project** is designed to spread awareness and understanding of essential digital skills required in today’s world. This project focuses on educating users about safe and effective use of technology, the internet, and digital tools.

It aims to bridge the digital divide by helping individuals develop basic to intermediate digital competencies such as:
- Using the internet safely
- Understanding online communication tools
- Awareness about cybersecurity and privacy
- Effective use of digital platforms for learning and productivity
