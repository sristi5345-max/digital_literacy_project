# Stay Safe Online: Cyber Safety Guide for College Students in India

A simple and practical checklist to help students stay protected from online risks, scams, and digital fraud.

---

## 1. Basic Online Safety Tips
- Create strong passwords using a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols, and avoid reusing them.
- Turn on two-step verification (2FA) for important accounts like email and social media.
- Do not open links or download files from unknown or suspicious sources.
- Be careful about what personal details you share online, especially on public platforms.
- Regularly update your phone, laptop, and apps to fix security issues.
- Always sign out when using public or shared devices.

---

## 2. Banking & UPI Security
- Never disclose your UPI PIN, OTP, or banking details to anyone under any circumstances.
- Double-check payment requests and scan QR codes carefully before sending money.

---

## 3. Reporting Cyber Issues
- Report any cybercrime or suspicious activity on the National Cyber Crime Portal: cybercrime.gov.in  
- Call the Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930 for immediate assistance.
- Act quickly in case of fraud to minimize loss and prevent further damage.

---

## Final Reminder
Stay cautious while using the internet. Always verify before you trust, protect your personal information, and report anything unusual without delay. Staying aware is the key to staying safe online.
